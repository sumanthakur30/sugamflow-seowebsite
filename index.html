QUESTIONS

What teams usually ask first.

What is SugamFlow?

A multi-tenant cloud workspace for billing, stock, customers, and the industry desks included in your plan. Patients and students do not get a public directory on this website.

Does every business get CRM and a clinic?

No. Modules come from the subscription assigned to the tenant. CRM is a separate desk. Clinic, lab, pharmacy, wholesale, and school screens appear only when that plan includes them.

How does GST billing work?

Invoices use the amount collected on the bill. Print and PDF stay on the signed-in invoice. e-Invoice and e-way bill run only when a GSP is configured. This site does not invent IRN numbers.

Where are the prices?

Commercial terms are assigned per tenant. The pricing page explains that on purpose and does not list a public rupee table.
