SugamFlow

Doctor OPD Management Software

SugamFlow gives clinic doctors a signed-in OPD desk: today’s queue, consultation, and e-prescription. It is staff software for a known tenant, not a public “find a doctor” marketplace.

Reception and visits

Reception creates encounters and can store a TPA/insurer memo on the visit. Collecting money uses the existing payment path; the memo does not change what is due.

Doctor desk

Doctors open today’s list, consultation, and prescription print/share. Optional WhatsApp reminder links use the existing wa.me pattern — there is no silent SMS blast from this website.

What is not included

Teleconsultation video rooms and a consumer appointment marketplace are not released as public SugamFlow products.
