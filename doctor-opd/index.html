CLINIC

Less paperwork. More time for patients.

Polyclinic and clinic types get reception, doctor OPD, e-prescription and visit billing on a shared patient master. In-house pharmacy and lab referrals stay on the same tenant.

Doctor OPD is the consultation desk (today board, Rx, referrals). Reception and visit billing sit on the same clinic tenant.

Front desk

Reception and queue TV

Appointments, queue and visit billing on reception. A clinic queue-display route is built for a waiting-room screen.

OPD

Doctor today board

Consultation pad: diagnosis, examination, history, tests and advice. Follow-ups and referral lists to pharmacy and path lab.

Rx

Printable prescriptions

Prescription PDFs plus shop-owned print, clinical and advice templates. Not a paper-only note.

Billing

Visit and service bills

Encounter billing for OPD, attached pharmacy and lab. Split payments and refunds on the healthcare billing APIs.

Records

Patient timeline

Tenant-wide patient master and an encounter timeline / complete history report. Dashboards stay after sign-in.

Hospital

IPD is hospital-only

Wards, nursing, rounds, ER and IPD billing exist for HOSPITAL tenants. Clinic and polyclinic stay OPD-first. Hospital is not self-serve registration.
