SugamFlow

GST billing software

SugamFlow billing creates GST invoices from the amount actually collected on the bill. It does not invent IRN, ARN, or e-way numbers when those services are not connected.

Invoices

Orders and healthcare collections print GST-aware invoices. Inclusive GST uses the selling price, not an invented MRP. Staff can print A4/thermal/PDF and use the WhatsApp-oriented share action.

Compliance screens

Tenants that enable them get GSTR-oriented summaries, reconciliation helpers, and e-Invoice / e-Way workflows. Push to a GSP is refused when the GSP base URL is not configured — that is intentional.
