Wholesale ERP for distributors

A distributor is not running a retail counter. On a wholesale subscription, staff raise sales orders, send delivery challans, and collect against buyer credit. That desk is separate from retail billing, and it is not included on every plan.

The operating problem

Goods leave on a challan, a beat or a delivery run, and payment often arrives later. Putting that work on a retail POS hides dues, dispatch status and the difference between an order and a tax invoice.

What the wholesale pack includes

When the pack is enabled, staff use a dayboard, sales orders, delivery challans, delivery runs, beat plans, a bill desk and collections. Party rate contracts and buyer credit limits sit on the customer. A tax invoice can carry GSTIN and transport fields. e-Invoice generation runs only when that GST integration is turned on for the tenant.

What stays private

The signed-in wholesale workspace is not a public URL. This page does not list live stock, party ledgers or invoice files. Retail, clinic and school work remain separate desks on the same company login.

Questions

Is wholesale the same screen as retail billing?

No. Retail billing and the wholesale desk are separate workspaces. Sales orders, delivery challans, beat plans and collections appear only when the wholesale pack is assigned to that tenant.

Can I open live orders from this page?

No. This is a public description. Staff sign in to the private application. Customer orders, prices and stock are not published here.

Does every SugamFlow plan include wholesale?

No. Wholesale is a capability pack. Retail, pharmacy, clinic, laboratory and school desks stay on their own configuration. Ask for a quote for the pack you need.
