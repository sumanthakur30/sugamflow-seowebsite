SugamFlow features

This list describes capabilities that exist in the current SugamFlow product. What a given business can use depends on business type, plan modules, and staff roles.

Commerce and stock

Retail and pharmacy counters share the same operational spine.

  • Product catalog and barcode-assisted billing
  • Stock, batches, expiry-oriented views, transfers, and purchase/GRN
  • Customers, suppliers, dues, and returns
  • GST invoices, print, PDF, and WhatsApp-oriented share

Healthcare desks

Enabled on clinic, lab, pharmacy, and hospital tenants.

  • Reception and OPD encounters
  • Doctor queue, consultation, and e-prescription
  • Pharmacy dispense queue
  • Pathology booking, worklists, and report publish
  • IPD / ER screens on hospital tenants

Finance and reports

Ledgers, expenses, AR/AP ageing, collection desk, owner dashboards, and a reports centre live behind sign-in. They are not public datasets.

Not claimed here

SugamFlow does not claim a consumer doctor-booking marketplace, guaranteed ChatGPT ranking, or certifications that are not published on the Security page.
