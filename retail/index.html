SugamFlow

Retail shop management software

SugamFlow retail tenants run a cloud counter: catalog, stock, purchase, GST invoices, and party dues with role-based staff logins.

Daily counter

Cashiers create orders, apply GST, collect cash or online amounts (including split where the bill supports it), and print or share the invoice. Owners use dashboards and reports after sign-in.

Stock and purchase

Products, stock adjustments, locations/transfers where enabled, and supplier GRN keep the shelf aligned with the bill.
