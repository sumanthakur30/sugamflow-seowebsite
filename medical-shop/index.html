SugamFlow

Medical Shop Management Software

SugamFlow is medical shop management software for Indian pharmacies and medical counters that need catalog, stock, purchase, GST billing, and collections in one signed-in workspace.

Medicine and product management

Staff maintain a product catalog (names, packs, tax, barcodes). Optional AI product capture helps draft a product from a photo or barcode; a person still reviews before it is used on a bill.

Inventory, stock, and purchase

Stock supports quantities, batches, and near-expiry views used by medical counters. Purchase orders and GRN receive supplier stock. PO OCR import can draft lines from a supplier document when the AI module is enabled.

Sales, billing, and GST

Counter sales create orders and GST invoices from the selling price collected — the same amount the customer is asked to pay. Returns and dues follow the existing order/payment screens.

Suppliers, customers, and payments

Supplier and customer/party records stay inside the tenant. Collection supports cash, online, partial due, and split cash-plus-online on supported bills. Invoices can be printed or shared through the WhatsApp-oriented print action.

Reports

Sales, stock, and GST-oriented reports run in the signed-in reports area. They are not published on this website.
