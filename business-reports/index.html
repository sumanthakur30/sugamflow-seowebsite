SugamFlow

Business reports

SugamFlow includes owner dashboards, sales analysis, stock reports, and healthcare operational lists. They require a staff login. The application path /reports is disallowed for crawlers.

Report categories

Typical signed-in reports cover sales, GST summaries, stock ageing/near-expiry, party dues, and clinic/lab operational queues. Exact menus follow the tenant’s modules and the user’s role.
