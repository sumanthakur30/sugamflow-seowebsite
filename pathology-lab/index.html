Orders

Booking and accession

Register tests, accession samples and drive a worklist. Camps and barcode settings sit in the same module.

Catalog

Tests, profiles, samples

Test master with parameters, profiles, sample types and pricing. Rate cards for referrers.

Bench

Department boards

Hematology, biochemistry, microbiology, histopathology and other department boards plus QC and NABL screens.

Release

Report PDF, not a public dump

Release from the lab desk. Patients use an OTP portal; doctors have a separate portal; a verify link exists. Marketing pages do not list reports.

Money

Lab payments and settlement

Path-lab payments hub, referrer commissions and settlement. Same tenant isolation as other shops.

Links

Clinic → lab

Polyclinic doctors raise path-lab referrals. Instruments and ecosystem (FHIR/ABHA) screens are present; treat ABHA as sandbox-style, not a live national claim.
