SugamFlow

Pathology Lab Management Software

SugamFlow includes a pathology lab workspace for labs that book tests, move samples through accession and department worklists, and publish reports to a patient who already has a link and OTP — not to the open web.

Lab operations

Path Lab screens cover test master, booking, payments, accession, department worklists (for example biochemistry or hematology when enabled), validation policies, report templates, QC, and referral/commission tools used by that tenant.

Patient report access

Patients do not browse a public list of reports. A shop can enable a patient portal that sends an OTP for that shop. Those /lab-report URLs are noindex and disallowed in robots.txt.

In-clinic labs

A polyclinic tenant can order lab work from the care flow so reception and doctors are not re-entering the same patient on a separate LIS. Isolation remains per tenant.
