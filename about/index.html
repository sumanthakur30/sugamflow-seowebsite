About SugamFlow

SugamFlow is a SaaS platform designed to help businesses manage operations, billing, inventory, payments, customers, suppliers and reporting. SugamFlow supports multiple business categories including medical shops/pharmacies, pathology labs, doctors/OPD, polyclinics, retail and other configurable businesses.

Who it is for

SugamFlow is built for retail shops, wholesale depots, polyclinics, pathology labs, medical stores, and schools that want one login model for owners and staff instead of stitching together separate billing, stock, and clinic tools.

  • Retail and pharmacy counters that need GST invoices, batches, and expiry control
  • Wholesale depots that run sale, challan, invoice, and credit together
  • Clinics that connect reception, OPD queue, e-prescription, pharmacy, lab, and IPD
  • Schools that manage admission, fees, attendance, exams, and parent updates

How it is delivered

The product is a browser-based cloud application. Each business (tenant) has isolated data. Staff sign in with role-based access rather than sharing a single password. Authenticated dashboards are not published as marketing pages.
