Questions

Is CRM the same screen as shop billing?

No. CRM is its own signed-in desk. Billing, stock, and clinic work stay in the operations workspace. A configured conversion can create a shop customer, a school inquiry, or a field-force record from a lead.

What does a lead store?

A lead has a title, contact or company, phone, email, source, pipeline stage, owner, priority, and an optional amount in INR. Tasks and a timeline sit on that record.

Does winning a lead create an invoice?

Not by itself. Collections and GST invoices are created in the billing workspace after the customer exists there.
