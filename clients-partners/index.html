Questions

Why is a business missing?

A registration stays private until the organization is active and approved. Suspended, demo, and manually hidden organizations are not listed. Phone, email, and tax details are never shown.

Does sending an enquiry open a login?

No. The form stores a message for the SugamFlow team. It does not create a shop, a user, or a partner login.

Partnership or client enquiry

This saves an enquiry for the SugamFlow team. It does not open an account or publish your organization.
