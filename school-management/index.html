SCHOOL

Same platform identity. Campus desks are not the shop POS.

SCHOOL is a real business type: Super Admin can onboard a school tenant, staff use the same login model, and school website domains are registered here. Fees, attendance and exams are not screens in shop-management-ui — those belong to the school workspace.
