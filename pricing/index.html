Pricing and plans

SugamFlow is sold as a subscribed SaaS workspace. Published rupee prices are not listed here because commercial terms are assigned per tenant and can change. Ask the team for a current quote.

How plans work

A Super Admin assigns a platform subscription to a tenant. That snapshot drives which modules appear (for example clinic, lab, pharmacy, wholesale, or school) and which limits apply. Feature services do not keep a second unofficial price list.

Trial and demo

You can request a demo from the homepage or start a trial registration when offered. Full GST and outlet setup is collected at registration, not on the first demo form.

What you are not seeing

This page does not display fake “starting at ₹X” figures, invented customer counts, or discount timers.
