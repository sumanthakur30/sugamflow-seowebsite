Catalog

Medicine master

Search and import from the platform medicine list, plus shop custom medicines. Composition and schedule fields live on the product.

Stock

Batch, expiry, FEFO

Batches and expiry dates on inventory. Near-expiry report. FEFO reserve when selling from stock-service.

Counter

Pharmacy POS

Dedicated pharmacy counter route plus walk-in prescription compose. Multi-unit (strip/tablet) packaging on medical, pharmacy and polyclinic types.

Dispense

Prescription queue

Clinic-origin prescriptions land on a dispense queue. Standalone shops still bill walk-ins without a doctor desk.

Compliance

Drug licence & mode

Shop record stores a drug licence number. Pharmacy compliance can be OFF, SOFT or STRICT from effective-config.

People

Patients, not only customers

Party list uses patient wording. Sales returns and GST stay on the same finance/GST screens as retail.
