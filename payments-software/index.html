Payments and split collection

SugamFlow records how a bill was collected: cash, online, partial due, or split cash-plus-online on supported documents. The authenticated /payments application route is not this page and is not in the sitemap.

Collection

Cashiers enter the amounts they actually took. Split payment is used on bills that require both cash and online legs (including some IPD bills). Dues remain visible on the order until settled.

What we do not claim

This page does not claim a specific payment-gateway brand, MDR rate, or UPI certification unless published elsewhere by SugamFlow.
