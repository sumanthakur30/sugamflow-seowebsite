GST billing software

Good GST billing is boring: the print matches the collect, and compliance jobs do not invent government numbers.

Inclusive tax

When a shop sells on inclusive GST, the payable amount is the selling price, not an MRP invented by software.

e-Invoice

SugamFlow can run e-Invoice workflows when a GSP endpoint is configured. If it is blank, push is refused. That protects the books.
