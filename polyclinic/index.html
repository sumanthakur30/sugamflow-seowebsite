SugamFlow

Polyclinic management software

A polyclinic tenant on SugamFlow is meant to run front desk, doctors, pharmacy, and lab as one care flow instead of three disconnected counters.

Connected desks

Reception, doctor queue, e-prescription, pharmacy dispense, and path-lab orders can share the same patient/customer record inside the tenant. Hospital-typed tenants additionally see IPD and ER boards.

Billing

Visit charges, pharmacy bills, and lab bills use the platform payment and GST tools already used by shops. There is not a second secret billing engine on this marketing site.
